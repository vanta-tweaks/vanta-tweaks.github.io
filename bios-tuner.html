BIOS Tuner

Your motherboard hides hundreds of settings from its own menu. BIOS Tuner reaches them and tunes them for gaming, for your exact CPU and board.

What it does

It reads every setting in your BIOS straight from the firmware, including the ones the menu never shows you. Then it picks the right value for each one for your CPU and your board, shows you every change, and applies them in one go.

Why it makes a difference

Out of the box, your BIOS is set up to save power and play it safe, not to game. Tuned for gaming, your PC reacts faster: latency improves significantly, and games feel smoother and more responsive.

Safe to use

It saves a backup of your BIOS settings before it changes anything, and one click puts them all back. Anything that could matter to an anti-cheat or your temperatures asks you first. You choose how far to go: Safe, Recommended or Max.

Always getting better

We update BIOS Tuner all the time, with support for more boards and new tweaks as we find them. Every update is included with your key.

Will it work on my PC?

It works on desktop PCs with an AMI BIOS, which is most boards from ASUS, MSI, Gigabyte and ASRock. Most laptops lock their BIOS. The free PC Check tells you for sure.
