What our apps do to your PC

Our apps change settings in Windows, your drivers and your BIOS to make games run better. Each app shows you what it will change before it does anything, saves a backup first and can put your old settings back.

Changing system settings always carries some risk, and every PC is different. Some settings, like the ones our apps mark as removing hardware protection, can make a PC run hotter or less stable. You choose what to apply. We're not responsible for problems caused by settings you chose to apply, to the extent the law allows. This doesn't limit any rights you have as a consumer under the law.

Our apps don't touch game files while you play, and we don't change Secure Boot. Anti-cheat systems can change their rules at any time, so we can't promise how a game will treat your PC's settings. Our apps warn you about the settings known to matter to anti-cheats.
