Core Tuner

Keeps your game's busiest threads on your fastest cores. Pick your games once and it runs from your taskbar.

Get Core Tuner

What it does

When one of your games starts, it finds the threads the game leans on most and keeps them on your fastest cores. The app shows every change, and nothing stays changed after the game closes.

Light

A small app in your taskbar. It only wakes up when one of your games starts.

Anti-cheat

It never touches game files. For games with strict anti-cheat, like VALORANT or FACEIT, it warns you first.
