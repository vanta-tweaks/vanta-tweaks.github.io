Latency Lab

Free. Measures your game before and after any tweak. It changes nothing.

Download Latency Lab

What it measures

FPS, 1% and 0.1% lows, PC latency (in Reflex games), stutters, GPU temperature and clock, CPU speed and throttling, and how long Windows makes your game wait.

How it works

Start a test, switch to your game and play. It records three runs and compares them with your last test, so you see what a tweak really did.

Works on

Windows 10 and 11, any game. It asks for admin rights to read Windows' own performance data.

Windows warning?

Click More info, then Run anyway. New apps get this message.
